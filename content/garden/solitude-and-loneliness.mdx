Same empty room, different door. Loneliness is being locked in; solitude is having chosen to enter.

What separates them seems to be **agency**, not company. I can be lonely at a party and entirely at ease alone on a train.

The long version lives in [[A Short Defense of Solitude]]. It also depends on tolerating [[Boredom]] and on how well I can hold [[Attention]] without an audience.
