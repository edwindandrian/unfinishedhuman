Friendships rarely end. They thin. Nobody decides; the replies just get slower.

The antidote is almost embarrassingly small: send the first message. Say *this made me think of you*. It works because it is a form of [[Attention]] made visible.

Full argument in [[What We Owe Our Friends]].
