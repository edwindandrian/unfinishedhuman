Attention is the one thing I can give that I cannot get back. Most of what I call "free time" is really a decision about where it goes.

Two observations so far:

- Attention degrades quickly under [[Boredom]]-avoidance. The reflex to fill every gap is the same reflex that kills the good thoughts.
- Attention is a form of care. Paying it to a [[Friendship drift|friend]], or to an ordinary afternoon, changes what the afternoon *is*. See [[The Weight of Ordinary Days]].

Still unresolved: whether attention can be trained directly, or only protected, as in [[Slow reading]].
