The root idea of this whole journal: a person is a process, not a product.

If that is true, then incompleteness is not a flaw to be fixed but the shape of the thing. The practical consequence is a different relationship to my own opinions. They are drafts.

Developed at length in [[On Being Unfinished]]. The daily-practice side of it is in [[Habits]].
