## Review

I return to this book whenever I am impatient with myself, which is often. Rilke's advice to a stranger is not really about poetry. It is about how to endure being unresolved.

## Reading notes

- Short enough to reread annually; I have, three times.
- The letter on solitude is the one I lend to friends.

## Favorite passages

> Have patience with everything unresolved in your heart and try to love the questions themselves.
