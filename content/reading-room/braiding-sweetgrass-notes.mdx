## Review

This book changed how I think about *receiving*. We are taught that gratitude is a feeling. Kimmerer proposes that it is closer to a practice, and that gifts create obligations of reciprocity rather than debts.

It is also simply beautiful prose, patient and exact, from someone who has spent a lifetime looking closely at moss.

## Reading notes

- The chapter on the serviceberry is worth the price of the book alone.
- She treats language as a way of seeing: verbs for living things, not nouns.

## Favorite passages
