## Review

I first read this at nineteen and found Meursault cold. Reading it again, older, I find the world around him colder, because it insists that he feel on schedule.

The book is not really about indifference. It is about the strange violence of being required to narrate yourself in approved terms.

## Reading notes

- The two halves of the novel mirror each other: the sun in the first, the courtroom in the second.
- Notice how often other characters tell him what he *must* be feeling.
- It takes an afternoon to read and a month to leave.
