---
title: "Memory is a draft"
date: 2026-11-12
category: the-inner-world
excerpt: "Every time you remember something, you edit it."
---

Every time you remember something, you edit it. The past is not an archive; it is a document that is open on your desk, and you keep making small corrections without noticing. Which means the version of your childhood you are most certain about is probably the most revised.
