---
title: "The Bakery at Six"
date: 2026-09-12
place: "Home"
excerpt: "An ordinary observation: the same twelve people, the same queue, and what it means to be a regular."
---

I have started going to the bakery at six, before work, mostly for the excuse to be outside. The queue is almost always the same twelve people, and none of us has ever exchanged more than a nod.

And yet if one of them is missing, I notice. Last week the man in the grey cap was absent two days running, and I caught myself worried. We do not know each other's names. We know each other's *orders*.

There is a kind of belonging that requires no introduction. I find it quietly sustaining.
