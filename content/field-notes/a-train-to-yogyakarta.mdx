---
title: "A Train to Yogyakarta"
date: 2026-10-05
place: "Java, Indonesia"
excerpt: "Eight hours of rice paddies, vendors, and the particular intimacy of strangers sharing a window."
---

The economy carriage smells of fried shallots and warm vinyl. A woman across from me is peeling a mandarin in one long, unbroken spiral, and I find I cannot look away from the craft of it.

Outside, the paddies are an impossible green, with a thin mist still resting on them. Every few minutes a village swings into view, a mosque, a motorbike waiting at a crossing, a child waving with her whole arm.

Somewhere past midday the man beside me begins, without preamble, to tell me about his daughter's exams. I understand perhaps two thirds of it. It does not seem to matter. He is not asking for anything except an audience, and I am, for once, content to be only that.

By the time we arrive, I have learned nothing useful and feel oddly well informed.
