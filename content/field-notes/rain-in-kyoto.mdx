---
title: "Rain in Kyoto"
date: 2026-06-21
place: "Kyoto, Japan"
excerpt: "A wet afternoon in a temple garden and a lesson in not fixing the weather."
---

It rained the entire afternoon, and I spent the first hour resenting it. I had planned photographs. The moss garden was supposed to be lit.

Then I sat on the wooden veranda with everyone else who had given up, and listened. The garden did not look better in the rain so much as *more itself*, darker and softer, every surface speaking.

A monk walked past with an umbrella and did not appear to consider the weather a problem at all. I made a note to stop treating mine that way.
